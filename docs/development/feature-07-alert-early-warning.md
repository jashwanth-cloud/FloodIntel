# Feature 07: Alert & Early Warning Intelligence

## Overview
This feature aggregates intelligence signals to provide explainable alert assessments.

## Signals
- IMD Weather Observations
- Heavy Rainfall ML (Feature 02)
- Rainfall Forecast (Feature 03)
- Risk Intelligence (Feature 01)
- Historical Satellite Evidence (Feature 06)

## Alert Levels
- NORMAL
- WATCH
- WARNING
- SEVERE

## Data States
- LIVE
- PARTIAL
- HISTORICAL
- DEMO
- UNAVAILABLE
- ERROR

## Limitations
- Intelligence aggregation, not a replacement for official emergency warnings.
